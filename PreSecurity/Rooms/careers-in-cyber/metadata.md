# Careers in Cyber

## Room Information
- **Room URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/careers-in-cyber
- **Module:** Introduction to Cyber Security
- **Difficulty:** Very Easy
- **Estimated Time:** 20-30 min
- **Access:** Free

## Description
This room explores the wide range of career paths available in cybersecurity. From penetration testing to SOC analysis, security engineering to management roles like CISO, students learn what each role entails, the skills required, and how to get started.

Understanding the cybersecurity career landscape helps students chart their own learning journey. The room also covers industry-recognized certifications (Security+, CEH, OSCP) and practical advice for building a professional portfolio.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify major cybersecurity roles and their responsibilities
- Understand career progression paths in security
- Learn about key certifications (CompTIA Security+, CEH, OSCP)
- Get practical advice for entering the field

## Key Tools and Technologies
- TryHackMe platform
- LinkedIn
- Certification bodies (CompTIA, ISC2, Offensive Security)

## Prerequisites
- None

## Expected Outcomes
Students will have a clear understanding of cybersecurity career options, required certifications, and actionable steps to begin their journey in the field.
