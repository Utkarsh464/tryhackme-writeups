# Networking

## Definition
Networking is the practice of connecting computers and devices to share data and resources. Networks range from small Local Area Networks (LANs) within a home or office to Wide Area Networks (WANs) spanning continents. Key components include routers, switches, firewalls, and transmission media (copper, fiber, wireless).

## Why It Matters
Cybersecurity is fundamentally about protecting data in transit. Attackers exploit network protocols, misconfigured devices, and weak segmentation. Understanding how networks are built and how data flows is essential for threat detection, firewall rules, and penetration testing.

## Key Points
- LAN vs WAN: scope and latency differences
- Router: forwards between networks (Layer 3)
- Switch: forwards within a network (Layer 2)
- Firewall: filters traffic based on rules
- Hub vs Switch: broadcast vs directed forwarding

## Common Interview Questions
1. What is the difference between a router and a switch?
**Answer:** A switch connects devices within the same network; a router connects different networks.
2. What is a default gateway?
**Answer:** The router a device uses to reach networks outside its own subnet.
