# Module 1: Intro to Cyber Security — Quick Reference

## Key Concepts
- **Offensive Security**: Ethical hacking to find and exploit vulnerabilities (red team, penetration testers)
- **Defensive Security**: Protecting systems by monitoring, detecting, and responding to threats (blue team, SOC analysts)
- **CIA Triad**: Confidentiality (secrecy), Integrity (accuracy), Availability (accessibility)
- **Penetration Testing Methodology**: Reconnaissance → Scanning → Exploitation → Privilege Escalation → Reporting
- **Career Paths**: SOC Analyst, Penetration Tester, Security Engineer, Forensic Analyst, GRC Analyst, CISO
- **Bug Bounties**: Ethical hackers paid for reporting vulnerabilities (HackerOne, Bugcrowd)
- **GDPR/Compliance**: Legal frameworks for data protection and privacy

## Key Terms
- **Red Team**: Attacks systems to test defenses (offensive)
- **Blue Team**: Defends systems against attacks (defensive)
- **Purple Team**: Collaboration between red and blue teams
- **White Hat**: Ethical hacker with permission
- **Black Hat**: Malicious hacker without permission
- **Grey Hat**: Hacker who may break laws but with good intentions
- **Zero-Day**: Vulnerability unknown to the vendor, no patch available
- **Patching**: Fixing vulnerabilities with software updates
