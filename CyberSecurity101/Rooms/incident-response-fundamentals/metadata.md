## Description

Incident Response Fundamentals teaches the structured, systematic approach to handling cybersecurity incidents. When a security breach occurs, organizations need a well-defined process to contain the damage, eradicate the threat, and restore normal operations. This room covers the complete incident response lifecycle as defined by NIST Special Publication 800-61 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide), the most widely adopted framework in the industry. The four-phase lifecycle begins with Preparation: building an incident response plan, assembling a Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT), acquiring necessary tools and technologies, establishing communication channels, and conducting training and exercises. The Detection and Analysis phase covers identifying Indicators of Compromise (IoCs), receiving and triaging alerts from various sources (SIEM, EDR, user reports, threat intelligence), analyzing the scope and impact of confirmed incidents, and documenting evidence. Learners explore different categories of IoCs: atomic indicators (IP addresses, email addresses, URLs), computed indicators (file hashes), and behavioral indicators (unusual network patterns, authentication anomalies). The Containment, Eradication, and Recovery phase covers short-term containment (isolating affected systems), long-term containment (applying temporary fixes), threat eradication (removing malware, closing backdoors, patching vulnerabilities), and recovery (restoring systems from clean backups, monitoring for recurrence). The Post-Incident Activity phase covers lessons learned meetings, incident documentation, evidence retention, and security posture improvements. The room also covers communication protocols during incidents: when to notify management, legal counsel, law enforcement, affected customers, and regulatory bodies. Practical exercises include classifying indicators, triaging incidents by severity, making containment decisions, and conducting a post-incident review. Understanding incident response is essential for any cybersecurity professional, as the ability to respond effectively to security incidents can mean the difference between a minor disruption and a catastrophic breach.

## Objectives
- Understand the NIST incident response lifecycle
- Identify and classify Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)
- Triage security incidents by severity and impact
- Make containment and eradication decisions
- Conduct post-incident reviews and lessons learned
- Understand communication and legal obligations during incidents
