# Room: Security Principles

## URL
https://tryhackme.com/room/securityprinciples

## Description
This room introduces the foundational security principles and frameworks that form the intellectual backbone of the cybersecurity field. Learners will explore the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity, availability) — the three pillars of information security — and understand how they apply to real-world scenarios such as data breaches, ransomware attacks, and system outages. The room covers the AAA framework (authentication, authorization, accounting), explaining how access control systems verify identity, grant permissions, and track usage. Learners will also study defense in depth, the principle of least privilege, separation of duties, and the concept of risk management including threat modeling and risk assessment methodologies. The room introduces major security frameworks and standards such as ISO 27001, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and COBIT, explaining how organizations use these to structure their security programs. Additional topics include security policies, governance, compliance requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS), and the shared responsibility model in cloud computing. By working through interactive scenarios, learners will apply these principles to make security decisions and evaluate the security posture of fictional organizations. This room is ideal for anyone preparing for entry-level security certifications such as Security+ or beginning a career in security governance, risk management, or compliance.

## Objectives
- Explain the CIA triad and provide examples of threats to each pillar
- Differentiate between authentication, authorization, and accounting
- Describe defense in depth and how multiple security layers protect assets
- Apply the principle of least privilege to access control scenarios
- Understand risk management terminology (threat, vulnerability, risk, impact)
- Identify the purpose of major security frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST CSF)
- Recognize common compliance requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS)
