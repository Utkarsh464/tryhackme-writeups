# Room: Public Key Cryptography Basics

- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/publickeycryptographybasics
- **Description:** An in-depth exploration of public key cryptography, covering the mathematical foundations and practical applications of asymmetric encryption. This room explains RSA key generation, the Diffie-Hellman key exchange protocol, digital signatures, and the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) that underpins internet trust. Learners will understand how certificate authorities issue and validate digital certificates, how certificate chains establish trust, and how public key cryptography enables secure communication without prior shared secrets.
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Time:** ~1 hour
- **Tier:** Premium
- **Objectives:**
  - Explain the mathematical principles behind RSA encryption
  - Understand the Diffie-Hellman key exchange mechanism
  - Describe how digital signatures provide authentication and non-repudiation
  - Understand the role of Certificate Authorities and PKI
  - Identify components of an X.509 certificate
  - Validate certificate chains and understand trust models
- **Tools:** OpenSSL (conceptual)
- **Concepts:** RSA, Diffie-Hellman, digital signature, PKI, CA, X.509, certificate chain
