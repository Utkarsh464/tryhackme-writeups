# Room: Cryptography Basics

- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/cryptographybasics
- **Description:** An introduction to the fundamental concepts of cryptography. This room covers the history of encryption from classical ciphers like Caesar and Vigenere to modern symmetric and asymmetric algorithms. Learners explore how encryption protects confidentiality, how keys are managed, and the critical distinction between encryption, encoding, and hashing. Practical exercises reinforce understanding of how cryptography secures data at rest and in transit.
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Time:** ~1 hour
- **Tier:** Free
- **Objectives:**
  - Differentiate between encryption, encoding, and hashing
  - Understand the principles of symmetric encryption using a shared key
  - Understand the principles of asymmetric encryption using public/private key pairs
  - Identify common encryption algorithms (AES, RSA, DES, 3DES)
  - Explain the role of key length in cryptographic strength
  - Recognize the importance of cryptographic nonces and IVs
- **Tools:** None required (may use OpenSSL)
- **Concepts:** Symmetric encryption, asymmetric encryption, cipher, key, AES, RSA, DES, block cipher, stream cipher, nonce, IV
