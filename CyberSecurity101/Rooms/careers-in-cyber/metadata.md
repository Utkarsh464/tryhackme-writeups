# Room: Careers in Cyber

## URL
https://tryhackme.com/room/careersincyber

## Description
The cybersecurity field offers a diverse range of career paths, each with unique responsibilities, required skills, and progression opportunities. This room provides a comprehensive survey of the cybersecurity job market, helping learners understand the roles available and how to prepare for them. The room covers entry-level positions such as SOC analyst (tier 1 and 2), cybersecurity analyst, and junior penetration tester, explaining the day-to-day responsibilities, key tools used, and typical career progression. Mid-level and senior roles including security engineer, incident responder, threat intelligence analyst, security architect, and security manager are explored in detail. The room also examines leadership roles such as CISO (Chief Information Security Officer) and the skills needed to reach executive levels. For each role, learners will learn about relevant certifications such as CompTIA Security+, CEH, OSCP, CISSP, and CISM, and understand which certifications are most valued for different career tracks. The room provides guidance on building a resume and LinkedIn profile, networking within the security community, attending conferences (DEF CON, BSides, RSA), and gaining practical experience through CTFs, bug bounty programs, home labs, and open-source contributions. Learners will also explore alternative paths including security consulting, independent bug bounty hunting, and cybersecurity teaching and mentoring.

## Objectives
- Identify at least 10 distinct cybersecurity roles and their primary responsibilities
- Compare the SOC analyst career path with the penetration testing career path
- Understand the certification landscape and which certifications align with specific roles
- Develop a personal career plan with short-term and long-term goals
- Learn how to build a professional network and online presence in the security community
- Identify resources for gaining practical experience including CTFs, labs, and open-source projects
