# Careers in Cyber — Tools

This room focuses on career planning rather than technical tools. The following platforms and resources are recommended for career development:

## Job Search and Market Research
- **CyberSeek:** Interactive career pathway tool showing the U.S. cybersecurity job market, including supply and demand data, common job titles, and certification requirements.
- **LinkedIn:** The primary professional networking platform for cybersecurity professionals. Used for job searching, networking, following industry leaders, and showcasing experience and certifications.
- **Indeed / Glassdoor:** General job search platforms with salary data, company reviews, and skill requirements for specific roles.

## Hands-On Practice Platforms
- **TryHackMe:** Gamified cybersecurity training platform with structured learning paths, virtual rooms, and hands-on labs covering both offensive and defensive topics.
- **Hack The Box:** Advanced penetration testing platform with realistic machines and challenges. Suitable for intermediate to advanced learners.
- **PentesterLab:** Focuses on web application security and penetration testing exercises with progressive difficulty.
- **PicoCTF:** Free, beginner-friendly CTF platform created by Carnegie Mellon University.

## Certification Bodies
- **CompTIA:** Provides Security+, CySA+, and other entry-level certifications widely recognized in the industry.
- **OffSec:** Provides OSCP, OSWP, OSED, and other highly respected offensive security certifications requiring rigorous hands-on exams.
- **SANS Institute:** Provides high-quality training and GIAC certifications across all security domains, though at a premium cost.
- **(ISC)²:** Provides the CISSP certification, widely considered essential for senior security roles.
- **ISACA:** Provides CISA, CISM, and CRISC certifications focused on audit, security management, and risk management.
