# Careers in Cyber — Concepts

## SOC Tier Structure
Security Operations Centers organize analysts into tiers based on skill level and responsibility. Tier 1 analysts monitor alerts and perform initial triage, determining whether an alert is a true positive or false positive. Tier 2 analysts conduct deeper investigations, contain incidents, and perform forensic analysis. Tier 3 analysts proactively hunt for threats, develop detection rules, and handle advanced persistent threats.

## Penetration Testing Methodology
A structured approach to testing security controls. The methodology includes reconnaissance (gathering information about the target), scanning and enumeration (identifying open ports and services), gaining access (exploiting vulnerabilities), maintaining access (establishing persistence), and covering tracks. Pentesters must also produce professional reports documenting findings and remediation recommendations.

## Certification Pathways
Certifications validate knowledge and are often required by employers. Entry-level certifications (Security+, GSEC) establish foundational knowledge. Technical certifications (OSCP, GPEN, GCFE) demonstrate specific hands-on skills. Advanced certifications (CISSP, CISM) are aimed at experienced professionals and often required for senior and management roles. Certifications should be chosen based on career goals rather than accumulated arbitrarily.

## Incident Response Lifecycle
A structured approach to handling security incidents. The NIST SP 800-61 framework defines four phases: preparation (building IR capability), detection and analysis (identifying incidents), containment, eradication, and recovery (stopping and removing threats), and post-incident activity (lessons learned and reporting). Each phase requires specific skills and tools.

## GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance)
A domain of cybersecurity focused on policy, risk management, and regulatory compliance rather than hands-on technical work. Governance establishes security policies and oversight. Risk management identifies and mitigates business risks. Compliance ensures adherence to laws and regulations. GRC professionals bridge the gap between technical teams and business leadership.

## Bug Bounty Hunting
The practice of finding and reporting security vulnerabilities in third-party applications and networks in exchange for monetary rewards. Platforms like HackerOne and Bugcrowd connect researchers with organizations. Successful bug bounty hunters combine technical skills (web application testing, reverse engineering) with persistence and methodology. It can range from a side hobby to a full-time career.
