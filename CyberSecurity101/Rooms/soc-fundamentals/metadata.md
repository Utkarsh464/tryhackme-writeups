## Description

SOC Fundamentals provides a comprehensive introduction to Security Operations Centers (SOCs), the teams responsible for continuously monitoring and defending an organization's information systems. A SOC is the central command post for cybersecurity operations, where analysts monitor networks, endpoints, and applications for signs of malicious activity, investigate alerts, and coordinate incident response. This room covers the complete SOC ecosystem: team structure, roles and responsibilities, tools and technologies, processes and procedures, and metrics and reporting. Learners explore the typical SOC tier structure: Tier 1 analysts triage incoming alerts, determine their validity, and escalate confirmed incidents. Tier 2 analysts conduct deeper investigations, perform threat hunting, and develop mitigation strategies. Tier 3 analysts are subject matter experts who handle complex incidents, perform advanced malware analysis, and develop new detection rules. SOC managers oversee operations, manage staffing, and report to leadership. The room covers essential SOC tools: Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) platforms for log aggregation and correlation, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) for endpoint visibility, Security Orchestration Automation and Response (SOAR) for automated workflows, threat intelligence platforms (TIPs) for contextual threat data, and ticketing systems for incident tracking. SOC processes covered include: alert triage and prioritization, ticket creation and management, escalation procedures, shift handovers, and communication protocols. Learners practice triaging sample alerts, classifying them by severity and type, and making appropriate escalation decisions. The room also discusses SOC maturity models, key performance indicators (KPIs), and the challenges of running an effective SOC, including alert fatigue, staffing shortages, and keeping pace with evolving threats.
