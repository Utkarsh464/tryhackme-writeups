# Module 13: Build Your Cyber Security Career — Rooms

## 1. Security Principles
- **Room:** Security Principles
- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/securityprinciples
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Time:** ~1 hour
- **Tier:** Free
- **Description:** Covers the foundational security concepts that underpin all cybersecurity work: the CIA triad, AAA (authentication, authorization, accounting), defense in depth, least privilege, risk management, and security frameworks such as ISO 27001 and NIST.

## 2. Careers in Cyber
- **Room:** Careers in Cyber
- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/careersincyber
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Time:** ~1 hour
- **Tier:** Free
- **Description:** Explores the full spectrum of cybersecurity career paths from SOC analyst to CISO. Each role is broken down by daily responsibilities, required technical skills, relevant certifications, salary expectations, and typical career progression timelines.

## 3. Training Impact on Teams
- **Room:** Training Impact on Teams
- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/trainingimpactonteams
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Time:** ~1 hour
- **Tier:** Free
- **Description:** Examines how cybersecurity training programs improve team performance and organizational resilience. Topics include awareness campaigns, phishing simulations, tabletop exercises, purple teaming, skills matrices, and methods for measuring training return on investment.
- **URL:** https://tryhackme.com/room/trainingimpactonteams

## Module Summary
Module 13 shifts focus from technical skills to the professional and human dimensions of cybersecurity. Across three free rooms, learners will explore the foundational security principles that guide all security work, survey the diverse career paths available in the field from SOC analyst to CISO, and understand how training programs impact team effectiveness and organizational resilience. This module is ideal for learners who are planning their career trajectory or preparing to enter the cybersecurity job market.
