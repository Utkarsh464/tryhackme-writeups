# Module 13: Build Your Cyber Security Career

## Name
Build Your Cyber Security Career

## Description
This module shifts focus from technical skills to the professional and human aspects of a career in cybersecurity. While many training paths concentrate exclusively on technical competence, success in the cybersecurity field also depends on understanding core security principles, navigating the wide range of career opportunities available, and recognizing how training and skill development impact team effectiveness. This module begins with Security Principles, where learners explore foundational concepts such as the CIA triad (confidentiality, integrity, availability), defense in depth, the principle of least privilege, risk management, and security governance frameworks. These principles form the intellectual backbone of every security decision, from access control policies to incident response strategies. The second room, Careers in Cyber, surveys the diverse landscape of cybersecurity roles including security analyst, penetration tester, SOC analyst, security engineer, incident responder, threat intelligence analyst, security architect, and chief information security officer. Learners will explore the typical responsibilities, required skills, certification paths, and career progression for each role, helping them identify which path aligns with their interests and strengths. The final room, Training Impact on Teams, examines how organizations build and measure the effectiveness of cybersecurity training programs. Topics include security awareness training for non-technical staff, skill development pathways for technical teams, tabletop exercises, red team vs. blue team drills, simulation-based learning, and methods for assessing training ROI. Learners will understand how continuous training improves detection and response capabilities, reduces human error, and strengthens an organization's overall security posture. By the end of this module, learners will have a clear picture of the cybersecurity profession and a roadmap for planning their own career development.

## Objectives
- Explain the CIA triad and its application to real-world security scenarios
- Describe defense in depth and least privilege as core security strategies
- Identify the major roles in cybersecurity and their associated responsibilities
- Evaluate career paths based on personal interests, skills, and goals
- Understand how security awareness training reduces organizational risk
- Describe methods for measuring training effectiveness and team readiness

## Prerequisites
- Completion of technical modules (Modules 1–12) recommended

## Outcomes
Upon completing this module, learners will be able to articulate core security principles and apply them to organizational scenarios, describe the major career paths in cybersecurity and identify steps to pursue them, and explain how structured training programs improve team performance and organizational security. This knowledge prepares learners to make informed decisions about their professional future in the field.
